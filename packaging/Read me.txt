Kiln
====

Kiln converts files from one format to another on Windows 10 and 11, and
makes files smaller.

Starting
--------
Open Kiln from the Start menu, or run kiln-app.exe in this folder. Drop
files on its window, or choose Add files. A wheel of the formats they can
become opens. Pick one, and the new file is saved next to the original.

From a terminal, kiln.exe does the same work, for example:
    kiln convert photo.png jpg
"kiln --help" lists the commands.

Helper apps
-----------
FFmpeg comes with Kiln, in the ffmpeg folder. Documents, office files and
e-books need a free helper app: Pandoc for documents, LibreOffice for office
files, spreadsheets and slides, and Calibre for MOBI and AZW3. A file that
needs one says which, and Settings > Helper apps can install or find it.

Where Kiln keeps things
-----------------------
Settings:                  %APPDATA%\Kiln\settings.toml
Log and crash reports:     %LOCALAPPDATA%\Kiln\logs
Results not saved yet:     %LOCALAPPDATA%\Kiln\held, and a hidden .kiln-held
                           folder on the drive of the file they came from
Windows' crash dumps:      %LOCALAPPDATA%\Kiln\CrashDumps (installer only)

Uninstalling Kiln leaves these, so a later install keeps your settings. To
remove them, delete the Kiln folders in %APPDATA%, %LOCALAPPDATA% and
%TEMP%, and any .kiln-held folder at the top of a drive, after uninstalling.
PRIVACY.md lists everything Kiln keeps and what it sends.

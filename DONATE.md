# Supporting Kiln

Kiln is free. If you'd like to help pay for the time it takes, there are two
ways:

- **[GitHub Sponsors](https://github.com/sponsors/chancrisp):** a monthly or
  one-time amount, through your GitHub account.
- **[Ko-fi](https://ko-fi.com/chancrisp):** a one-time tip, with a card or
  PayPal, no account needed.

Nothing in Kiln depends on it.

Other ways to help: report a bug, ask for a format, or tell someone about Kiln.
